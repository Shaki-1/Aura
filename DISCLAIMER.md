# Disclaimer

AURA is an accessibility assistance tool and prototype.

## Not Medical Software

AURA is not medical software. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor medical conditions.

## Not Certified Accessibility Software

AURA is not certified accessibility software. It is intended to demonstrate local accessibility support concepts such as visual filters, profiles, and experimental reading-area detection.

## User Responsibility

Users and organizations should evaluate whether AURA is suitable for their needs and environment before relying on it for accessibility workflows.
